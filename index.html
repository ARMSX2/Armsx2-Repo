ARMSX2 iOS

PlayStation 2 games on iPhone and iPad. Needs JIT.

LiveContainer SideStore Feather

Those only do anything on the device with the app installed.

https://ios.armsx2.net/apps.json

Scan to open this on your phone. The buttons work there.

Before you start

  • iPhone or iPad on iOS 17 or later. Without JIT most games are too slow to play.
  • You provide your own PS2 BIOS and game files. Neither is included.
  • Performance varies by device and by game. Some run perfectly, others crawl or will not boot. Check the compatibility list first.

Two channels

ARMSX2 iOS

The tested release. Start here.

ARMSX2 Nightly

Fresh code, most days. New features arrive here first, and so do the bugs.

They install side by side, with separate save states, memory cards and settings. Trying a nightly leaves your stable install untouched.
